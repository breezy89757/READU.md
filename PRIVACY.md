# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-10-04

**READU.md** is a Markdown reader and editor for Windows. It works locally on your device.

1. **Data Collection**: The developer does not collect, receive, or store any of your data. READU.md has no analytics, telemetry, or advertising.
2. **Local Operation**: The files you open and edit stay on your device. Settings are saved in the app's local data folder (`settings.json`).
3. **Optional AI Summary**: AI Summary is off until you configure it with an AI endpoint, API key, and model. When you ask for a summary, the content of the current document is sent to the endpoint you configured (for example Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, or a compatible gateway); how that service handles it is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Your API key is encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) so only your Windows account can read it.
4. **Internet Access**: Apart from AI Summary requests to the endpoint you configured, READU.md does not send data over the internet. Its rendering components are bundled with the app. Images or other content that a document links to on the web are loaded from those sites when you view the document, as in a browser.
